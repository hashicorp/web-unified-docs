## Configuration alignment checklist

Before installing or upgrading, confirm that:

- The proxy listener port in `worker.config` matches `worker.service.proxy.targetPort`.
- The ops listener and `worker.service.ops.targetPort` remain `9203`, and `tls.ops.disabled` agrees with the listener TLS configuration.
- The `auth_storage_path` matches `worker.persistence.authStorage.path` when auth storage is enabled.
- The `recording_storage_path` matches `worker.persistence.recording.path` when recording storage is enabled.
- The `public_addr` matches the endpoint other Boundary clients or workers can actually reach.
- Placeholder activation tokens, cluster IDs, and upstream addresses have been replaced.

## Troubleshooting

### Worker crash-loops after enabling auth storage

If the worker pod crash-loops after you enable auth storage, confirm that `auth_storage_path` in `worker.config` matches the mounted PVC path set by `worker.persistence.authStorage.path`. A mismatch between the two means the worker cannot read or write its identity material at the expected location.
