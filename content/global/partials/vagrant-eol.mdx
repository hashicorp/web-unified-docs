<Note>

HCP Vagrant is being <a href="/hcp/docs/vagrant/hcp-vagrant-eol">deprecated</a>.  Customers using Vagrant boxes hosted on the HCP Vagrant registry must find a new provider and re-host their boxes.  As of October 1, 2026, new boxes can no longer be created in HCP Vagrant. Existing box deployments will continue to operate until the end of operations date of December 31, 2026.  After that time, all deployments will be decommissioned.

<a href="https://github.com/hashicorp/vagrant">Vagrant CLI</a> has been open-sourced under the MIT license to allow the community to continue to use and improve Vagrant.

</Note>
