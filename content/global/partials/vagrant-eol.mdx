<Note>

HCP Vagrant is being <a href="/hcp/docs/vagrant/hcp-vagrant-eol">deprecated</a>.  Customers using Vagrant boxes hosted on the HCP Vagrant registry must find a new provider and re-host their boxes.  As of December 14, 2026, new boxes can no longer be created in HCP Vagrant. Existing box deployments will continue to operate until the end of support date of March 15, 2027.  After that time, all deployments will be decommissioned.

</Note>
