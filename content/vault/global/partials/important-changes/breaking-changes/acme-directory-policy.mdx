### PKI ACME `sign-verbatim` now filters unverifiable certificate attributes ((#acme-sign-verbatim-filtered))

| Change       | Affected version | Vault edition
| ------------ | ---------------- | -------------
| Breaking     | 2.1.2+           | All

The semantics of the `sign-verbatim` policy for PKI ACME directories have
changed. Previously, `sign-verbatim` issued certificates for any identifier
the client could prove ownership of, including unverifiable attributes such as
SANs or extensions. This permissive behaviour is now named
**`sign-verbatim-unsafe`**.

The updated `sign-verbatim` policy enforces filtering: certificates that
include attributes that cannot be validated through ACME ownership proofs —
such as additional SANs or custom extensions — are now **rejected**.

#### Recommendation

If your ACME clients request certificates with additional SANs or extensions
beyond the identifiers verified by the ACME challenge, you must either:

- Transition to a role based directory policy where the role permits the 
  additional names (recommended).
- Remove those attributes from your certificate requests to remain on
  `sign-verbatim`, or
- Explicitly set `default_directory_policy` to `sign-verbatim-unsafe` in your
  [ACME configuration](/vault/api-docs/secret/pki/issuance#set-acme-configuration)
  to restore the previous permissive behaviour.  

Note that `sign-verbatim-unsafe` carries the same risks as the
[Sign Verbatim](/vault/api-docs/secret/pki#sign-verbatim) endpoint and should
only be used if absolutely necessary.
