<Note title="Restore not completed on return">

  The restore command exits as soon as Vault validates and accepts the snapshot
  file, not when the restore action completes. The full restore action runs in the
  background to tear down all subsystems, swap the database, and then restart the
  subsystems.  Do not resume regular operations until you see "post-unseal setup
  complete" and "vault is unsealed" in the server logs.

</Note>
