# View policy enforcement results for Stacks

<Badge text="Beta" />

When you add [policy sets](/terraform/cloud-docs/policy-enforcement/manage-policy-sets) to a Stack, HCP Terraform enforces those policy sets on every deployment. HCP Terraform displays the policy enforcement results in the UI for each deployment run. Depending on each policy's [enforcement level](/terraform/cloud-docs/policy-enforcement/manage-policy-sets#policy-enforcement-levels), policy failures can also stop the deployment and prevent Terraform from provisioning infrastructure.

Stacks currently supports [Terraform policy](/terraform/cloud-docs/policy-enforcement/define-policies/terraform-policy) only. Support for Sentinel and OPA is not available for Stacks.

HCP Terraform only evaluates policies for successful plans. For Stacks, Terraform policy evaluations can occur at setup time, plan time, or apply time depending on the policy configuration:
- Setup evaluations can occur before providers and modules are downloaded during init
- Plan time evaluations occur after the plan is complete
- Apply time evaluations occur after the apply is complete

## View Policy Results

To view the policy results for a Stack deployment:
1. Go to your Stack and navigate to the **Runs** page.
1. Click a deployment run to view its details.

## Override Policies

You need the [manage policy overrides](/terraform/cloud-docs/users-teams-organizations/permissions/organization#manage-policy-overrides) permissions to override failed Terraform policies in Stack deployments.

To override failed policies, ensure that your user has the "manage policy overrides" permission, then go to the deployment run details page and click the **Override and Continue** button.

### Terraform policy enforcement levels

When policies with an `advisory` enforcement level fail, HCP Terraform will notify the user in the deployment results and continue the deployment.

When policies with a `mandatory_overridable` enforcement level fail, HCP Terraform will halt the deployment. Users with the manage policy overrides permission can override these policy failures and continue the deployment.

When policies with a `mandatory` enforcement level fail, HCP Terraform will halt the deployment. You cannot override failures of `mandatory` policies.
