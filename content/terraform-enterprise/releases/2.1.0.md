## Highlights

1. Terraform Enterprise now supports Valkey 9.0 as a drop-in replacement for Redis. You can connect to an external Valkey data store using the same connection settings as Redis.
1. Terraform Enterprise now provisions the read-only Site Auditor role automatically through your identity provider. Configure a SAML attribute or role, or map a SCIM group, to grant and revoke cross-organization Site Auditor access without manual admin action. When SCIM is enabled, SAML auditor provisioning is skipped. The role is Terraform Enterprise only and cannot be granted to service accounts. Refer to the SAML and SCIM configuration guides for details.
