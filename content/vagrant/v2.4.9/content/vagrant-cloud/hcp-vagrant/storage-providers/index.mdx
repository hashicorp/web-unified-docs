# Migrate to a storage provider

@include "@global/vagrant-eol.mdx"

HCP Vagrant Registry is being deprecated. Use these guides to migrate your boxes and metadata from HCP Vagrant Registry to another storage provider.

Each guide walks through preparing your boxes, moving them to the target provider, and updating your Vagrantfiles to reference the new location.

## Available guides

Select a storage provider to get started. Additional guides will be added to this section over time.
