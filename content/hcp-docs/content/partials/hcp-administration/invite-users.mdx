<Note>

If [Single Sign-On](/hcp/docs/hcp/iam/sso) is enabled, manage users through the configured identity provider instead. Manual user invitations are not available.

</Note>

1. Log into [HCP Portal](https://portal.cloud.hashicorp.com/) and navigate to your organization dashboard.
1. Click **Access Control (IAM)** in the sidebar.
1. Click **Users**, then click **+Invite user**.
1. Enter the user's email address, toggle **Assign this user a role**, and choose the scope for the role assignment.
1. Select a role and click **Add role assignment**. Refer to [User permissions](#user-permissions) for information about the roles you can assign.
1. Repeat these steps to invite additional users.

### Resend a pending invite

To resend an invite to a specific user:

1. Click **Access Control (IAM)** in the sidebar.
1. Click **Pending invites**.
1. Click the **Actions** menu for the user you want to resend an invite to and click **Resend invite**.
