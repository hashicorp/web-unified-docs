# What is pages

A **what is** page is a landing page for a product or sub product. Use these
types of pages to describe how an entity works and its purpose. What is pages map
to the Diátaxis **explanation** category.

To start drafting, copy [templates/what-is.mdx](../templates/what-is.mdx).

## North star principles

Make sure that your what is content aligns with the following north star
principles.

### Optimize for SEO

Include strong keywords that resonate with users, page metadata, and a question
title that mimics what users may type into a search engine. Refer to
[metadata.md](metadata.md) for meta description patterns.

### Build trust with the user

Use industry terms and technical detail that match practitioners' vocabulary to
explain how our products work. Avoid flowery language or hyperbole and provide
explanations that are precise, concise, and clear. This helps gain credibility
with your target audience.

### Lead with goals

Structure content around what users will achieve with the product rather than
around specific features. For example, instead of a heading for "Custom modules",
use "Standardize configurations" instead. Rather than leading with
product-specific jargon, the introduction pages should highlight the problems
users want to solve and then map product features to those goals.

## User journeys

Our goal is to create documentation for all audience types, but what is pages are
optimized for the following practitioners.

### New users and researchers

- This user is trying to understand why the product would meet their needs.
- May be studying for certification or is new to cloud infrastructure, in
  general.
- Has allocated time to read and absorb the page in its entirety instead of
  quickly scanning for terms to address an immediate need.
- Focused on "what" and learning patterns.
