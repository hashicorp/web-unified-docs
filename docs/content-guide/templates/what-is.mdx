# What is {product}?

One to three sentences describing the product and its main benefits. Start with
"{Product} is…" so that the definition is the first thing a reader and an AI
summary encounter.

> **Hands-on:** Try the [name of tutorial](link) tutorial.

## How does {product} work?

Explain how the product delivers the benefits named in the opening. Describe how
practitioners and other key user groups use the product at a high level. This may
involve defining the key user workflows and explaining how the product
communicates with other parts of the broader ecosystem, such as cloud providers,
CI/CD pipelines, and databases.

If your product is complex, briefly discuss use cases or provide an example
scenario that illustrates the product's benefits.

Include at least one visual aid to illustrate key workflows or data flows between
the product and its ecosystem. Refer to
[visual aids](../content-types/visual-aids.md) for diagram conventions and alt
text requirements.

## Why {product}?

List the main product benefits as bold lead-ins structured as action statements.
Under each, briefly explain the product features that help users achieve the
benefit. Link to the reference documentation for those features where possible,
and always provide context for product-specific jargon such as feature names.

Optionally, include an explanatory video if one exists for your product.

## Which edition of {product} is right for me?

This section is optional. Use it to explain your product's paid tiers or
editions and to link to pricing pages or more information. Omit it if editions do
not exist for your product, or if they have their own dedicated page.

Refer to
[paid offerings and pre-GA releases](../../style-guide/general/enterprise-releases.md)
for how to label edition-specific content.

## Get started

Optional. Link to the tutorials or quickstart pages that a new reader should
follow next.

## Community

Include links where users can ask questions, contribute to the product where
applicable, and submit bugs or feature requests.
