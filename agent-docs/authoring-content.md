# Authoring content

Guidance for writing and editing pages under `content/`. All of it lives in
`docs/` and is the source of truth for humans and agents alike. **Read those
files rather than relying on recall** — they change, and this page can drift
from them.

## Check the product before choosing a type

Products do not implement the content types uniformly. Some rename a type, some
add one the taxonomy does not have, and some do not use a type at all.

Check [docs/content-guide/products/](../docs/content-guide/products/index.md)
for your product **before** picking a type or copying a template. A product page
takes precedence over the global guidance for the differences it records, and
its mapping table names the template to use. An absent override is not a claim
that the product follows the global template — each product page says which of
the two it means.

## Mechanics that are easy to get wrong

- **Do not write the date metadata block.** `created_at` and `last_modified` are
  generated by the `pre-commit` hook. Refer to
  [create-new-page.md](../docs/content-guide/create-new-page.md).
- **A page does not publish until it is in the sidebar.** Add it to the
  product's `*-nav-data.json`.
- **Moving or renaming a page needs a redirect**, and some products need more
  than one.

## Product-local instructions

When a task is specific to one product or content set under `content/`, check
that product area for its own `AGENTS.md` and follow those local instructions
first. Path-specific review guidance lives in
`.github/instructions/*.instructions.md`.
